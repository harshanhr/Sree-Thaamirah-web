🥣 Simple 3-Minute Preparation
1
Mix Smoothly

Take 2 tablespoons (approx 30g) of Arogya Health Mix. Mix into 250ml of cool water or milk without lumps.

2
Simmer & Cook

Cook over a medium flame for 3 minutes, stirring continuously until the porridge thickens and turns glossy.

3
Sweet or Savoury Option

Sweet: Add jaggery, palm sugar, or honey. Savoury: Allow to cool slightly, add fresh buttermilk and rock salt!
